ZKE has been installed.

Open the Console locally:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward service/{{ include "zke.server.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.server.service.httpPort }} 8081:{{ .Values.server.service.podAccessPort }}

Open the Console and follow the first-run page to choose the global
administrator username and password.

Before enrolling a remote Cluster, set server.agentInstall.publicHTTPURL and
server.agentInstall.publicQUICAddress to endpoints reachable by that Cluster.
